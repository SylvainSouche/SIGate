about=SIGate adds new categories to QGIS's Data Source Manager for browsing, searching, and downloading geospatial data from national mapping agencies exposing Atom, WFS, ArcGIS REST, or WMS/WMTS services. Bulk downloads that are archives (zip, tar, or 7z, including split multi-part 7z archives) are extracted and added to the project directly; downloads that are plain files are added as a layer with no extraction step. An optional external 7-Zip executable is required only for sources that distribute data as .7z archives; .zip and .tar-family archives require no external dependency.
